“Hi, I'm Fahmida, also known as Wolf Widow in the gaming world. I'm a statistics graduate who loves finding patterns in data and finding bugs in games.”

I hold a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Statistics and I'm now doing an M.Sc. in Statistics at the University of Chittagong. My work there is data analysis and statistical modelling: turning raw numbers into insights, using Python, R, SPSS, STATA and MS Excel.

Gaming is my passion, and I turned it into a career by co-founding Raven's Claw in July 2025 with our CEO, Knight Wolf. Data analysis and QA have a lot in common: attention to detail, structure, curiosity and clear communication. I work remotely with international clients.

I'm looking for entry-level roles in Data Analysis / Data Science, QA / Game Testing, and statistics or research.
