About the Widow

I'm Wolf Widow: gamer, game tester and co-founder of Raven's Claw, a game and software QA company based in Bangladesh. I hunt the bugs that hide in the shadows so players never meet them.

I've been competitive for as long as I can remember, and I play to win. That same drive goes into every test pass. I'm disciplined enough to grind through long sessions, and I'm stubborn about tracking a bug down to the steps that trigger it.

I run with a pack. Alongside Knight Wolf, our founder & CEO, I protect the people I work with and the games we test. When I'm off the clock, you'll find me in Counter-Strike 2 and Rust.

What I Do

⚔

Functional Testing

I check every mechanic, menu and system against how it's meant to work, so the game does what it promises.

🜂

Exploratory Testing

I go off the beaten path and play the way real players do, looking for the edge cases nobody scripted.

Regression Testing

After every patch I re-hunt old ground to make sure fixed bugs stay dead and new builds don't break what worked.

Bug Reporting
